## When to Read?

While an idea is in the early seed stage and you are game planning as this book is focused on Phil Knight & Nike pre-IPO.

## My 2¢

I want to preface by saying, I read the whole book in like a week as the content is light and conversational. It's a fun read and they were supposed to make a movie but it never came to fruition.

There are a few different angles to try and distill down with this. The first being, this memoir focuses on the first 18 years of Nike (1962 - 1980 | Pre-Michael Jordan). This was also when Adidas was an absolute behemoth and had a 15 year head start… oh and Phil Knight was 25 years old. You can surmise the cards were stacked against Knight.

Another angle with this is Knight was an avid runner; he knew the product, he knew the limitations & he knew what runners actually wanted. For the first 8-ish years Nike (Blue Ribbon Sports at the time) was only selling Onitsuka sneakers… I think it is a testament to persevering in that success is in essence entirely dependent on a 3rd party product you have minimal control over.

To wrap this up, the first 45% of this memoir leads up to the tangible payoff where Knight and team brought the Nike Cortez (aka Forrest Gump shoes) to market in 1972. They did so with the shoe bearing the iconic swoosh logo and timed it up so athletes were wearing it at the Summer Olympics that were held in… you guessed it… Germany.

As a whole, enjoy reading about the early tribulations of building a business. You’ll most likely end up appreciating how the first 20+ years in this memoir set them up to run the table from the 80’s onward…

TLDR; read the book first then watch [Air](https://www.amazon.com/gp/video/detail/B0B8Q3JMCG?ref_=atv_dp_share_cu_r).
