Account - Growth Propensity Scoring. Tried to be clever with coming up with an acronym of this and thus A-GPS was what I went with.

I know only a handful of people will actually read the below so in 22 words it's this… Scoring algorithms you can plug into Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Grok Bot to analyze accounts and help you try and grow them.

## Why?

I wish I had a cool name for this like Fable, Astra, Dark Matter or something like that but the marketing budget is nonexistent so A-GPS (Version III) is what I got for y’all. I have built iterations of this at Alteryx in 2024 as well as at Cloudflare in 2025-2026 but with this version I aimed to make the logic more readily accessible and applicable for others to leverage.

To round off the why. Several years ago leadership asked, “How can you 10X your team's impact?” This was my approach to solving for this… It will most likely not 10X your team's impact, but what it will do is help get you into position for growth based off of data driven indicators and help your team establish a sense of where they should be focusing their time and energy.

## How it works

<SketchSplit src="/images/agps-quadrants-sketch.png" alt="Hand-drawn sketch of the P1 to P4 growth and spend quadrants" side="left">

The scoring is similar to golf, the lower the score, the higher the propensity for growth. Or for all your SQL peeps, ORDER BY.

The logic aims to be inclusive of data signals from GTM (Sales), Marketing, Customer Programs, Customer Success, Support, Pro-Services, Product and last but not least Situation & Timing. In total 35 different data elements are measured with 30% of the model's weight being attributed to GTM… aka ensuring it is focused on $ and ¢. Additionally 25% attributed to Post-Sales Teams as well as 25% to Situation & Timing. The remaining 20% of the weight allocated to Marketing | Customer Programs and Products.

Here are the keys I really want to drive home. The entire premise of this is centered on Growth (Growth Potential) and Customer Spend (ARR/NRR). Meaning customers who have both substantial growth potential and spend will be landing in the P1 (upper right) quadrant and customers who have low growth potential and low spend will be in the P4. Given how the scoring works, it will additionally keep your larger spending customers on your radar typically landing in the P3 quadrant.

## Where & When?

This is however more geared towards teams who have a larger volume of accounts in their portfolio, think 1:25, 1:50, 1:75 or greater ratios. Alternatively it can be helpful for Director / VP level individuals if they are looking to get a macro view of accounts in a given segment as I know we all love weekly forecast calls.

When first rolling this out; having a team call to provide transparency on how the accounts were scored, what was the logic as well as the call to action are extremely critical… Just like syllabus week where professors go over the curriculum in week 1 of a semester, you need to do the same when rolling this out.

Some other quick things pertaining to timing. If your team has large account ratios I would aim to have a quarterly refresh so they can investigate & validate, chart a course and action. Each account will take varying lengths depending upon its circumstances as well as how fast your team can operate. This will also give you time at EOQ to interject findings and or new logic into the scoring for the subsequent quarter.

## How to Roll it out?

<SketchSplit src="/images/agps-rollout-sketch.png" alt="Hand-drawn sketch of the A-GPS rollout timeline" side="right">

When rolling this out for the 1st iteration with your team, it's imperative that you have your full crew aware and present as well as whoever orchestrated tuning the scoring elements. I typically would allocate a full hour long call on a Monday or Tuesday and a full week before live roll out. Expect Questions, expect skepticism, expect early confusion and double down in your 1-2-1’s to listen to team concerns, ideas and get a gauge of their confidence.

Now as a leader once it's live, support and coach your team over the next 2 to 10 weeks but also have channels for feedback of what's working & whats not working. Coupling this via providing a standardized way to measure the teams impact on targeted accounts and accounts they were able to actually deliver, actions, impacts and results is critical.

Towards the end of your rollout (typically 10 to 12 weeks) having centralized feedback that can be cross referenced with data over a course of 90 days allows you to do a postmortem that will help shape and improve the logic of the next iteration of your rollout.

Lastly when you go to roll out say V2 of A-GPS, the team gets to see that their feedback was interjected into the logic as well as pick up on lessons learned and what actions drive the most impact for results they are aiming for.

## Scoring Algorithm

*…aka what you all came here to plug into your Claude, Grok, ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat.*

Before you use this. This is a framework, not a magic number. I highly encourage you to tune this for your book of business. Your customer spend ranges, deal sizes, risk appetite, sales motion and data quality should all shape it a bit differently. A $250K account is a behemoth at some companies and just another account at others. So take it, run it, then dial it in until your P1s look like accounts your team would actually bet on. Now go have fun and try it out.
